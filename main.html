Mental Health Conditions

Understanding symptoms, physiological effects, and affected demographics.

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Symptoms

Persistent sadness, loss of interest in activities, fatigue, feelings of worthlessness, trouble concentrating.

Effects

Sleep disturbances, changes in appetite/weight, chronic physical pain, lowered immunity.

Commonly Affects

Young adults (18–25), females, individuals with a family history of depression.

Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

Symptoms

Excessive worry, restlessness, muscle tension, difficulty controlling worry, irritability.

Effects

Rapid heart rate, gastrointestinal problems, insomnia, chronic muscle stiffness.

Commonly Affects

Adults aged 30–50, students under academic stress, working professionals.

Bipolar Disorder

Symptoms

Extreme mood swings ranging from manic highs (euphoria, hyperactivity) to depressive lows.

Effects

Disrupted circadian rhythm, impulse decisions, physical exhaustion post-mania.

Commonly Affects

Typically diagnosed in late teens or early adulthood (ages 15–25).

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

Symptoms

Flashbacks, nightmares, severe anxiety, intrusive memories, avoidance of triggers.

Effects

Hyperarousal, panic attacks, high stress hormone (cortisol) production, insomnia.

Commonly Affects

Survivors of trauma, disaster survivors, first responders, military veterans.

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

Symptoms

Unwanted repetitive thoughts (obsessions) and urge to perform routines (compulsions).

Effects

Physical exhaustion from repetitive tasks, skin breakdown (e.g., from over-washing hands).

Commonly Affects

Children, teens, and young adults; often begins in early childhood or adolescence.

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Symptoms

Inattention, executive dysfunction, hyperactivity, impulsivity, difficulty organizing tasks.

Effects

Restlessness, sleep disruption, difficulty regulating emotional responses.

Commonly Affects

Children, adolescents, and adults across all gender demographics.
